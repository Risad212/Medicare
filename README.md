# MediCare Hospital

MediCare lets visitors explore hospital services and doctors, request appointments, and access patient services. Hospital staff and doctors can sign in to manage their assigned work.

## View the demo

This repository does not have a public demo website. To try MediCare, open the demo URL provided by the person hosting it and use the demo login details they provide. If you do not have a demo URL or login, contact the site administrator.

## Using the website

### Visitors

- Browse the home page, hospital information, services, doctors, and blog.
- Open **Doctors** to find a doctor and view their specialty and availability.
- Select **Book Appointment**, choose a doctor, date, and available time, enter the requested details, and submit. You can request an appointment without signing in.
- Use **Contact** to send a message to the hospital.

### Patients

- Register or sign in using the options on the website.
- Open **My Profile** to review your profile and available appointments, lab information, and other patient records.
- Book an appointment from **Book Appointment**. Your appointment status will be updated by hospital staff or your doctor.
- Cancel an eligible appointment from your profile. If you booked without an account, use the cancellation link provided with your booking.

### Doctors

Sign in with the doctor account provided by the hospital. The doctor area shows your appointments and patient-related tasks. Depending on your access, you can update your profile, manage appointment status, review or create lab orders, and handle blood requests.

### Hospital staff

Sign in with the account provided by the hospital. The options available depend on your role. Authorized staff can manage appointments and patients, update website content, process laboratory work and billing, manage blood bank activity, and use other hospital modules enabled by the administrator.
